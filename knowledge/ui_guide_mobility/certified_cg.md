# Advanced: Certified CG Entry

Use the **Advanced: certified CG entry** vehicle source when you already have a certified
gross weight and centre of gravity (for example from a certified report) and want to enter
them directly.

## When to use it

This is an advanced path for certified CG data only. For normal use prefer the workbook
baseline; use certified entry when an authoritative external CG must be assessed as-is.

## What you must provide

- **Gross weight** and the **CG coordinates** (Xcg, Ycg, Zcg).
- The **geometry** (wheelbase, track) and the **axle limits**.
- A **source / reference label** — this is mandatory. It is recorded in the provenance line
  so every result is traceable back to the document the CG came from.

## Coordinate conventions

- **Xcg** — from the front axle, positive rearward.
- **Ycg** — from the centreline, positive to the right.
- **Zcg** — above ground.

## After entry

Once the certified vehicle is entered with its source label, "Run Analysis" becomes enabled.
The provenance line will show the method and the source reference you provided.
