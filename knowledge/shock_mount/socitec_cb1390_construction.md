# Socitec CB1390 Construction, Envelope, and Interfaces

**Chunk:** `shock_mount/socitec_cb1390_construction`
**Source:** SRC-CAT-01, `Socitec - CB1390.pdf`, page 1
**Grounding:** vendor catalogue; reference data, not project-specific approval

---

The CB1390 is an all-metal, multidirectional helical wire-rope isolator. The
standard catalogue construction is:

- cable: stainless steel; galvanized cable is available as the CBG option;
- retainer bars: aluminium alloy with SurTec finish;
- screws: zinc-plated alloy steel;
- inserts: stainless steel;
- all-stainless construction: CBSS option;
- standard loop count: eight loops.

The catalogue describes high damping, no ageing, corrosion resistance, and a
temperature range of -180 to +300 deg C. These are vendor claims and must be
confirmed for the ordered configuration and environment.

## Interface suffixes

The exact suffix defines the hole pattern in both retainer bars. The catalogue
offers combinations of 8.4 mm through holes, 90-degree countersunk holes, and
M8 inserts. Examples include no suffix, CM, CM2, IM, CIM, and IM2.

Never specify only `CB1390-20` or `CB1390-30` in a supplier enquiry. Ask the
supplier to confirm the complete part number, both bar interfaces, fastener
size, required engagement, orientation, and mounting clearances.
