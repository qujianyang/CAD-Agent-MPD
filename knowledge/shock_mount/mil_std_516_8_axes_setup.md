# MIL-STD-810H Method 516.8: Axes, Setup, and Documentation

**Source:** MIL-STD-810H, Method 516.8, method pages 516.8-16 and 516.8-22.
This summary is based on the project copy of `MIL-STD-810H.pdf`.

## Axes and directions

The method states that shock exposures should be selected from the life-cycle
environment and, as applicable, applied in both directions along each of the
three orthogonal axes. The item installation orientation therefore matters.

## Test preparation

The method calls for a documented test configuration and calibrated or
validated fixtures and instrumentation. A pre-test plan identifies the
procedure, configuration, shock level, duration, climate, and number of shock
applications. Visual inspection and operational checks are carried out before
testing.

## Project interpretation

The shock-mount tool's four deterministic load cases approximate the relevant
mounting directions for its rack model. They are an engineering selection
method, not a replacement for a physical three-axis qualification test.

## Use in answers

Use this source to explain why the mount count, orientation, attachment
configuration, and documented assumptions are necessary inputs.
