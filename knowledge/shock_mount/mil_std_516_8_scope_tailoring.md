# MIL-STD-810H Method 516.8: Scope and Tailoring

**Source:** MIL-STD-810H, Method 516.8 (Shock), method pages 516.8-1 to
516.8-2. This summary is based on the project copy of `MIL-STD-810H.pdf`.

## What the method covers

Method 516.8 addresses the ability of materiel to withstand mechanically
induced shocks that may occur during handling, transportation, and operation.
It also identifies mounting, packaging, and restraint as parts of the overall
protection system.

## Tailoring is required

The method states that the selected procedure, shock level, pulse duration,
number of shocks, and axes must be tailored using the item's life-cycle
environment profile (LCEP) and applicable requirements documents. Field data
and item-specific requirements are preferred over generic defaults.

## Project interpretation

The project treats a shock pulse such as 20 G for 11 ms as a specified design
input, not as a universal requirement for every mounted item. The shock
isolator tool evaluates the supplied pulse against the selected mount and the
project's engineering limits.

## Use in answers

Use this source to explain why the user must confirm the operational scenario
and why a shock requirement should be tailored. Do not use it to calculate
isolator stiffness or transmitted acceleration; use the deterministic shock
analysis tools for those values.
