# MIL-STD-810H Method 516.8: Functional and Transportation Shock

**Source:** MIL-STD-810H, Method 516.8, method page 516.8-4, Procedures I and
II. This summary is based on the project copy of `MIL-STD-810H.pdf`.

## Procedure I: Functional Shock

Functional shock evaluates whether an item retains physical integrity and
operational functionality during and after a specified shock. It is relevant
when the engineering question is whether installed equipment can remain
functional after a shock event.

## Procedure II: Transportation Shock

Transportation shock evaluates the response of an item and its restraint or
mounting system during transportation. The method describes use of measured or
synthetic terminal-peak sawtooth shock pulses for this procedure.

## Project interpretation

For equipment installed on wire-rope isolators in a rack or enclosure,
transportation shock is the closer conceptual match because the mount and its
restraint arrangement affect the response. A project may still specify a
functional-shock pulse as its engineering requirement; the applicable
procedure must be confirmed from the actual requirement document.

## Boundary

Method 516.8 is a test-method framework. This application performs a mount
selection and verification calculation; it does not certify that a physical
test has been performed or that a product is MIL-STD compliant.
